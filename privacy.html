🛡️ Privacy-First Guarantee

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026 · QuestTick Version 1.0.0

Summary in Plain English

QuestTick is an offline-capable, local-first browser extension. We do not collect, track, store, or sell any of your browsing activity, personal information, or game progress. Your checklists and item notes stay 100% inside your own browser.

1. Data Storage and Processing

All data generated while using QuestTick—including checked boxes, item notes, custom profiles, progress ratios, and the list of gaming wikis you visit—is saved exclusively in local browser storage (using the standard chrome.storage.local API).

This data remains solely on your physical device. It is never synchronized to QuestTick servers, never transferred to advertising networks, and never accessible by third parties.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

QuestTick strictly adheres to the Chrome Web Store Single Purpose and Minimal Permissions policies. We never collect:

  • No browsing history: We do not track websites, queries, or pages outside of calculating item marks on wikis you actively use.
  • No personally identifiable information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or device IDs.
  • No telemetry or analytics: There are no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, or third-party tracking pixels bundled inside the extension.
  • No tracking cookies: QuestTick never sets, inspects, or reads HTTP tracking cookies.

3. License Verification (QuestTick Pro)

QuestTick connects to the internet only and exclusively when a user voluntarily inputs a commercial license key to activate QuestTick Pro.

In that scenario, QuestTick communicates directly over an encrypted HTTPS connection with the official public Customer Portal API of our merchant of record, Polar.sh (https://api.polar.sh/v1/customer-portal/license-keys):

  • The license key entered by the user.
  • A generic platform label (e.g., "QuestTick Chrome - Windows") used by Polar to count concurrent active devices.

No personal browsing activity, checklist marks, or page URLs are ever transmitted during license verification. Polar's privacy practices are governed by Polar.sh Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions Justification

QuestTick requests only the minimum required Chrome extension permissions:

  • storage: Needed to persist checklist marks, custom notes, user settings, and save profiles locally.
  • alarms: Used for background scheduled re-validation of active Pro licenses once every 24 hours.
  • scripting: Used only when you explicitly click "Enable on this site" to inject checklist markers into unsupported gaming wikis.
  • activeTab: Used to display contextual page title and item completion counts in the popup menu.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full ownership of your data at all times. You can export or import a complete JSON backup of your checklist records from the extension's Settings Hub. If you uninstall QuestTick from your browser, all stored progression data is permanently erased immediately by Chrome.
