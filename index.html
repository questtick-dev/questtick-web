Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about QuestTick.

Does it work on Brave, Edge, Opera, or Vivaldi? +
Yes! QuestTick is built strictly to the Manifest V3 standard and is 100% compatible with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, and any Chromium-based desktop browser.
Will my checked items disappear when the wiki updates its table? +
No! Unlike fragile row-index tools, QuestTick extracts canonical cell texts and computes an FNV-1a hash fingerprint. Even if the wiki adds new columns, sorts prices, or inserts new rows, your marks stay locked to the exact same item.
Can I enable QuestTick on an obscure or custom gaming wiki? +
Yes! When you open the extension popup on any unlisted website, click the "Enable QuestTick on this site" button. QuestTick will instantly inject checkboxes into any checklist-style tables on that page.
Is there any subscription fee? +
No subscriptions whatsoever. The Free tier is free forever. QuestTick Pro is a single $6.99 one-time payment for lifetime access across your devices.
